# CoLazy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CoLazy ("CoLazy", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the CoLazy mobile app for iOS and Android (the "App").

If you have any questions, contact us at **team@colazy.com**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information:** your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are stored in hashed (scrambled) form by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password in plain text.
- **Profile information:** anything you add to your profile, such as a display name or profile picture.
- **Learning content:** goals you create, activities you complete, and the answers you submit. Depending on the activity, this can include text, code, quiz answers, puzzle solutions, drawings, photos, audio recordings, and video recordings.
- **Documents:** PDF files you upload so the App can create learning activities from them.
- **Support and error reports:** anything you choose to send us when you report a problem or contact support.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage information:** how you use the App. This includes activities you view, complete, skip, or like, your progress on goals, and how much time you spend in the App.
- **AI usage information:** how many AI requests are made for you and what they cost. We use this to apply the AI limits of your plan.
- **Device information:** your device type and operating system version.
- **Push notification token:** a token that lets us send you notifications, if you allow them.

### Information from app stores

- **Subscription information:** Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) handle all payments. Through our subscription provider, RevenueCat, we receive your subscription status, plan, trial status, and renewal and expiry dates. **We never receive or store your payment card or bank details.**

## 2. Device Permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings. Some features won't work without them.

| Permission | Why we ask for it |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take photos or record videos for activities or your profile picture |
| Photo library | To choose photos or videos from your device |
| Microphone | To record audio answers |
| Speech recognition | To turn your speech into text. This may be processed by your device maker's speech service (Apple or Google) |
| Notifications | To send learning reminders and updates |
| Files | To choose PDF documents to upload |

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and sign you in.
- Provide the App, including creating personalized learning goals and activities and giving feedback on your answers.
- Track your progress and show your learning stats.
- Manage your subscription, free trial, and AI usage limits.
- Send you notifications you have allowed.
- Answer support requests and fix problems.
- Keep the App and our users safe and prevent misuse.
- Understand how the App is used so we can improve it.

**We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.**

## 4. How We Use AI

To create activities and give you feedback, the App sends the relevant content to a third-party AI provider. This content can include your goal descriptions, your answers, and text from PDFs you upload. We currently use **Google Gemini**. We may also use **OpenAI** as a backup provider.

The AI provider uses this content only to send a response back to us, under its API terms. AI-generated content can be wrong, so please check anything important. **Please don't include sensitive personal information (such as health, financial, or government ID details) in content you submit.**

## 5. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in these cases:

**Service providers that help us run the App.** They may use your information only to provide their services to us.

| Provider | What they do for us |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Account sign-in, database, and file storage |
| RevenueCat | Subscription management |
| Apple and Google | App distribution, payments, push notifications, and speech recognition |
| Expo | App updates and delivering push notifications |
| Google (Gemini) and OpenAI | AI processing, as described in Section 4 |

**People you choose to share with.** If you link with a partner in the App, your shared goals and progress are visible to that partner. If you make a goal public, other users can see and copy it.

**Legal reasons.** We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or CoLazy.

**Business transfers.** If CoLazy is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 6. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores some information on your device, such as your sign-in session, settings, and cached content, so it works faster and stays signed in. This information is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

## 7. Third-Party Websites and Content

The App includes an in-app browser and can show embedded content such as YouTube or Vimeo videos. When you visit other websites or watch embedded content, those companies may collect information under their own privacy policies. We don't control their practices, so please review their policies.

## 8. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as we need it to provide the App.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days. We may keep some information longer only when the law requires it, or to resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their own policies.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- See the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct information that is wrong.
- Delete your account and personal information.
- Get a copy of your information in a portable format.
- Object to or limit how we use your information.
- Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
- Complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email **team@colazy.com** from the email address linked to your account. We will reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

**Subscriptions:** you can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings (iOS) or Google Play account (Android). Deleting the App or your CoLazy account does **not** cancel your subscription.

**Notifications:** you can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 10. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA, UK, and Similar Laws)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a place with similar laws, we process your information on these legal bases:

- **To provide our service (contract):** running your account, the App's features, and your subscription.
- **Legitimate interests:** keeping the App secure, fixing problems, and improving the App, where these interests are not outweighed by your rights.
- **Consent:** device permissions and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** when the law requires us to keep or share information.

## 11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database so that users can see only their own data. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 12. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may store and process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

## 13. Children's Privacy

The App is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age required by the law where they live. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email **team@colazy.com** and we will delete it.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the App. If you keep using the App after an update, the updated policy applies.
